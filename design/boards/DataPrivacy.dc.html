Source: UK GDPR Art 12(3) and the ICO's right of access guidance. Within one month of receipt, worked out the safe way (a day early). Up to two more months for complex or many requests, only if we tell the person within the first month. Pausing the clock while we wait for ID: ICO guidance allows it, and the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 puts it in law (check it has commenced).

Requests from anyone

"Download everything" serves the client's account holder. Everyone else comes here: registered managers, co-directors, referees, landlords, GPs named on forms, prospects and staff. Requests count however they arrive: email, phone, a message or a letter. Every staff screen has "Log a request". Types: access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, complaint. "Correct something we hold" on the client's Account page lands here too.

Export rules

  1. Consultants download single documents only; no bulk export.
  2. A bulk export needs approval by someone else, and appears here.
  3. Links expire after 24 hours. Every download is logged.
  4. Logins never appear in an export (locked).
  5. A client's own "Download everything" needs the mobile code when we hold their sign-in mailbox login.
Who may export is set per role on People and access (board 83).
