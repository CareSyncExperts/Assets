# Super admin: decisions that settle every conflict (4 November 2026)

These win over anything a board, email or spec section currently says. Rule ids refer to SP/superadmin/inventory.json.

## Decided by Care Sync (owner)
- aftercare-length: **12 months of aftercare is included in the full start-up package only.** Registration-only clients (CQC domiciliary care, supported living, CIW, RQIA) get their Registration File and a handover call at registration, and can buy aftercare as an add-on ("Aftercare, 12 months", price to set). Aftercare boards show only full start-up clients or clients who bought the add-on.
- review-platforms: **Google and Trustpilot, both offered; the client chooses.** Either can be switched off in super admin.

## Legal, reputational, promise-breaking
- review-no-gating: every registered client is invited once, at the moment of registration, whatever their feedback. Nobody is held back or steered. Worried clients get no SALES or growth messages until resolved; the review invitation is NOT held back. Locked rule (cannot be switched off).
- no-time-estimates: means **no promises about how long a regulator, buyer or decision will take**, and no "most people finish in N days". Form pages may say how long the page takes to fill in ("About 5 minutes"). Locked rule. Journey board's "Most people finish this stage in 5 days" and similar must go.
- tender-submit-margin and tender-timeline: Care Sync submits **2 working days before the buyer's deadline**. Draft to the client **10 working days before**, client approval **5 working days before**. Northshire (deadline Tue 1 Dec 12:00): draft to Kemi Tue 17 Nov, Kemi approves Tue 24 Nov, Care Sync submits Fri 27 Nov. Case study (Wed 11 Nov) and staff numbers (Mon 16 Nov) stay. Clarifications close Fri 13 Nov 17:00 (unchanged).
- nop-representation-deadline and appeal-deadline: worked out **the safe way: the day of service counts as day 1** (so 28 days ends 27 days after service), from the service date on the notice; if unsure, the earliest possible date. Labelled "worked out the safe way; confirm with a legal adviser". Sunrise: served Wed 21 Oct, legal deadline Tue 17 Nov (unchanged). Westbrook: notice of decision Mon 26 Oct, appeal window closes Sun 22 Nov (was 23 Nov). The client Cqc board example: served 2 Feb, deadline Mon 1 Mar.
- nop-internal-targets: draft by **day 14**, senior review and client approval by **day 20**, send by **day 21** (a week before the legal deadline). Alerts day 7, 14, 21, 25, then daily. Sunrise fits: senior review Mon 9 Nov (day 20), send Tue 10 Nov (day 21).
- break-glass and cover: **planned leave uses cover delegation**: when leave is booked, a manager approves cover for named clients and dates, including vault reveal for those clients, ending automatically. **Break-glass is for emergencies only**: approved by a super admin who is not the requester; if none can be reached, the person may self-authorise with a written reason. Always: 4 hours, alerts both super admins and the client the same day, reviewed within 1 working day. Client wording: "A senior member of the Care Sync team approved it." Daniel's Greenway request becomes cover delegation (Olu's leave), not break-glass.
- second-super-admin-approvals: needs the other super admin: price changes, discounts, permission changes, super admin invites, form versions, template and email versions with legal wording, retention schedule changes, early or unscheduled deletion, break-glass (unless emergency self-authorised). Routine deletions inside the approved schedule run automatically with 7 days' notice. **Setup mode**: before go-live, the first super admin may set things up alone; go-live needs two active, named super admins; from then on dual control is on. Anyone with permission may SUGGEST a price; only a super admin enters it.

## The journey told one way
- onboarding-form-reminders: gentle reminder after **2 days**, again after **4**, Grace calls on **day 6**. Then weekly until done. document-request-reminders: after **3 days**, then weekly. Third parties (landlord, referee): after **3 working days**, then weekly. Draft review: after **3 days**. Signature: after **2 days**, then the consultant calls.
- weekly-update-day: **Friday for every client**, by **16:00**. Rhythm: Monday review, Wednesday chase, Thursday second-person check, Friday send. One setting changes the day for all. The client cannot switch it off; they choose the channel.
- no-work-before-handover: kick-off and sessions are booked **after handover approval**, never at welcome.
- onboarding-gates-list: the 8 gates as on boards 8 and 13 (Company and directors, Identity, Office address evidence, Registered manager, Employment history, References, DBS, Professional registers). Form complete is a precondition, not a gate. CIW, RQIA and tender gates differ as on board 74.
- handover-approver: a **manager** approves (directors count as managers).
- id-dbs-retention: **proposed default, for the data protection lead to confirm**: identity document images and DBS certificate images are deleted once the regulator has made its decision; the Hub keeps a record (seen by, date, document type, DBS number and issue date). Everything else follows the retention schedule (Data and privacy board).
- resubmission-gate: the **7 items** on board 66. resubmission-refresh-ages: DBS under 12 months and references under 6 months at the resubmit date are **our rules**, labelled as such.
- identity-documents: 3 documents (as the forms ask). Mark RQIA "check" in the spec.
- growth-offer-rules: **one growth idea shown at a time**, no more than one new idea a month, never during a concern or refusal.
- quality-gate-second-person: **absolute**, cannot be relaxed.
- bid-no-bid-approval: the **client decides** whether to bid; **Care Sync accepts the work** (a director) when there is a capacity or same-lot question. Wording: "Accept the bid work".
- notification-channels: Phase 1: email, the Hub's bell, and **texts only for the introduction call and urgent legal deadlines**. WhatsApp is a later phase (shown as "Later", off). quiet-hours: **8pm to 8am** for automated emails and texts; legal deadline alerts and tender deadlines within 24 hours still go.
- review-invitation-moment: at registration, once, with one reminder after 7 days.
- worried-client-rules and unhappy-feedback-routing: goes to the **manager**, who calls within 1 working day (a director if the manager is involved) and shares it with the consultant with care.
- relationship-check-moments: one tap, three faces, at **three moments**: after onboarding, after submission, after the decision; then monthly in aftercare. Reports show "% happy", not out of 5.

- document expiry warning: **6 weeks** before expiry, for everything.
- inspection-readiness-timing: **month 4** (full start-up aftercare).
- form version approval: the onboarding lead drafts, the other super admin approves.
- stage-model: 11 stages (spec 4); spec 18.3's sub-steps are statuses inside them. Escalation: manager first; director for legal deadlines.
- stage-waiting-colours: "waiting on" amber after 5 days, red after 10. no-outbound-contact-red: 7 days. unanswered-message-red: 1 working day. watch-gap-red: 7 days.
- banned-words: "free" is not used in client messages; staff screens say "no charge to the client" or "included", never "free resubmission".
- client-sign-in: magic link, no password; known device stays signed in 30 days. **When Care Sync holds the login to the client's sign-in mailbox, a code to the client's mobile is needed on new devices and before signing, approving, downloading everything or changing contact details.** The mailbox connection never files Hub sign-in or e-signature emails.
- tender-form-sections: 15 (as board 48 and the real form). Portal passwords are never asked (Appendix D questions marked removed).
- connections: every supplier listed (see Data and privacy suppliers).
- roles: super admin is a flag on top of a role. Roles: manager, consultant (case owner), onboarding lead, client care, coach, sales, finance, data protection lead (a manager). Directors and managers see all clients; others see assigned and covered clients.
- demo world: remove "Referee 2 due later".
- sending-addresses: automated Hub messages from hello@caresyncexperts.co.uk (domain authenticated); personal messages from the staff member's Google Workspace mailbox.
- send-after-signing: 2 working days. rfi-reply-targets: our target is 4 working days before the regulator's date.
- Weekends: an internal target that falls on a weekend or bank holiday moves to the working day before (legal deadlines never move).
