## The principle: it works together
1. **Every rule is defined once** in the registry (board 80, Rules and promises; file SP/hub-canvas/project/registry.js for the design boards). Screens never hold their own copy of a promise, price, timing or threshold.
2. **Every setting shows where it is used**: client screens, staff screens, emails, automations, reports, with board numbers and email numbers, and how many clients a change would affect today.
3. **Every change is previewed, approved and dated**: draft, see the impact, the other super admin approves where decisions.md says so, choose "new clients only" or "everyone from a date", published with a version. Legal values are shown with their source and a lock; they are not editable.
4. **Nothing fails silently**: every failure (integration, automation, delivery, watch) shows on System health with what it affects and who is told.
5. **One decisions inbox**: the super admin Home shows every decision waiting for a super admin, and the nav badge equals that count.
